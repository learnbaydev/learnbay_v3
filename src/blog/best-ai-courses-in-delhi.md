## Glossary: 2026 AI Terms You Will See in Every Syllabus

:::note
**Quick answer:** The terms every 2026 AI syllabus should name are LLMs, RAG, fine-tuning, AI agents, MCP, A2A, vector databases, LLMOps and guardrails.
:::

- **LLM (Large Language Model):** A model trained on large text data that can generate and understand language, such as GPT-4o, Claude or LLaMA.
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):** A method where an LLM answers using relevant documents fetched at query time. Production-grade RAG adds re-ranking, evaluation and failure handling.
- **Fine-tuning:** Training a pre-trained model further on your own data. LoRA and QLoRA are low-cost fine-tuning methods.
- **Agentic AI:** AI systems that plan steps, call tools and act on a goal with limited human input.
- **Multi-agent frameworks:** Tools such as LangGraph, CrewAI and AutoGen that coordinate several agents.
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol):** An open standard that connects AI models to tools and data sources.
- **A2A (Agent2Agent protocol):** An open standard that lets agents from different systems talk to each other.
- **Vector database:** A database that stores embeddings for similarity search, such as Pinecone, FAISS or Chroma.
- **LLMOps:** Practices for deploying, monitoring, versioning and controlling the cost of LLM applications.
- **MLOps:** Practices for deploying and maintaining classical ML models in production.
- **Guardrails:** Checks that keep AI output safe, accurate and compliant. Essential in BFSI and healthcare.
- **Small language model (SLM):** A smaller, task-specific model that is cheaper to run than a general LLM.
- **Forward Deployed Engineer:** An engineer who builds and integrates AI systems inside a client’s environment.
