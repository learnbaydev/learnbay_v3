FAQs –

How to build an AI model?

Creating an AI model requires a well-defined problem statement, followed by crucial steps like data collection, algorithm selection, model training, and performance evaluation.

How to build Agentic AI projects?

To build Agentic AI projects, you need to follow the Think → Act → Observe → Repeat structure. It means that to build such projects, one should create a system that works with LLMs to plan, act, and execute different activities to meet the end goal.

Why do Agentic AI projects fail?

Many Agentic AI projects fail due to a lack of governance, poor data quality, security risks, costly investments, misaligned targets, agentic complexity, and a lack of skills.

What is the difference between Agentic AI and traditional AI?

Agentic AI reflects a goal-driven shift led by autonomous agents. On the other hand, traditional AI focuses on rule-based approaches to accomplish a task. Also, Agentic AI operates autonomously, without human intervention, unlike traditional AI models.

What is Agentic AI vs. Generative AI?

Generative AI is an AI version that creates new content, while Agentic AI is an advanced AI model that independently works without human intervention. GenAI is a creator that reacts to human prompts, while agentic models prefer proactive responses. GenAI models have low autonomy, unlike agentic systems with high autonomy levels.

Will artificial intelligence take away jobs?

Yes. Artificial intelligence will replace some stagnant roles, but it will mainly revamp, reshape, and augment several job roles for better yields.
