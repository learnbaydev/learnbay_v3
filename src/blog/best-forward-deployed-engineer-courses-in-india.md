## What Is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

:::note
**Quick answer:** The **Forward Deployed Engineer** meaning is simple. FDE full form is Forward Deployed Engineer, and it is the engineer who goes to the customer and makes an AI system that works in a demo work inside their real environment, with their data, their security rules and their users. The title comes from Palantir, and enterprises rolling out AI now hire for it directly.
:::

![Four layers of the Forward Deployed Engineer role: build, run, architect and client work, with most AI courses covering only the first.](https://d32and0ii3b8oy.cloudfront.net/adlearnbay/FdeRoleFourLayers191026.webp)

In a normal product team, you ship a feature and move on. As an FDE, you sit with the client, find out what is actually broken, build against their messy data, get it past their security review, and then hand it over so their team can run it.

## Three Red Flags Before You Pay for an FDE Course

:::note
**Quick answer:** Check three things before you pay. Does the syllabus go past build into run and handover? Does it end in a deployment you can open in an interview? And does anyone promise you a job?
:::

**Red flag 1: the syllabus stops at build.** GenAI, retrieval, agents, and then nothing about running it in production or handing it to a client. That is a Generative AI course with a new name on the certificate.

**Red flag 2: it ends in an exam or a video library.** A badge proves you know the words. An FDE interview asks what you deployed, what broke and what it cost to run.

**Red flag 3: a guaranteed job.** Nobody can guarantee one. Programs that lead with a guarantee usually have conditions buried in the contract.
