## What Does a Data Scientist Do in 2027, and Which Skills Matter Most?

:::note
**Quick answer:** A data scientist turns raw business data into predictions and decisions. In 2027, that means Python and SQL for data work, statistics and machine learning for models, and GenAI tools to speed up analysis. Employers also expect you to deploy a model and explain its results in plain business terms.
:::

On a normal day, a data scientist cleans messy data and builds models that predict outcomes such as loan defaults or product demand.

Here are the skills hiring managers test for in 2027 –

- Python and SQL to collect, clean and query data
- Statistics and hypothesis testing to check whether a result is real or just chance
- Machine learning, which means models that learn patterns from past data to predict new outcomes
- Deep learning and NLP (natural language processing) for images, text and speech
- GenAI and RAG, where RAG (retrieval augmented generation) lets a large language model answer from a company's own documents
- Deployment and MLOps, the practice of putting models into live use and keeping them accurate

Python appears in nearly three-quarters of AI job postings in India, and SQL or data engineering skills in more than half, as per [foundit Insights Tracker data](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/ai-roles-cross-2-9-lakh-in-2025-hiring-seen-up-32-in-2026-87532.htm) from 2026.

If you want the full step-by-step path, the [data science roadmap from beginner to specialist](https://www.learnbay.co/blogs/data-science-roadmap-beginner-to-specialist) lays out each phase. In short, start with Python and statistics, then move to machine learning, GenAI and deployment.

Hence, a course that stops at machine learning will leave a visible gap on your CV in 2027.
