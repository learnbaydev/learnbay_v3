## FAQs:

### What's the difference between a Generative AI course and an Agentic AI course?

**Ans.** The <b>best Gen AI courses online</b> teach you how to prompt AI models and create new content for various platforms. On the other hand, with an Agentic AI course, you learn how to build autonomous systems. In 2026, the best AI courses in India combine both, since agents are built on top of generative models.

### Do I need to know Python before enrolling in a Gen AI course?

**Ans.** It depends on the course and your goals. Programs aimed at non-technical leaders (like the Applied AI Practitioner Program) require no coding. Programs aimed at senior or tech professionals eager to build and deploy AI (like the SDE Master Program or the Developer Program) require at least a real-time knowledge of Python. Hence, you can opt for the courses that teach it from scratch. Yet, always check the stated prerequisites before enrolling in a course.

### Are Gen AI certifications valued by employers in 2026?

**Ans.** Certifications signal structured commitment, which matters. But in most technical hiring conversations in 2026, what actually differentiates candidates is portfolio evidence. In 2026, you must show your real-time experience in building AI models, projects you've built, problems you've solved, and systems you've deployed. The most valuable outcome of a Gen AI course isn't the certificate; it's the deployable AI projects you complete during the program.

### How long does it take to complete a serious Gen AI program?

**Ans.** Most comprehensive mentor-led programs run 6–12 months, designed for working professionals studying with flexible batches. Self-paced courses are faster, but career success rates for these courses are dramatically lower than structured ones — something to factor in when comparing apparent learning efficiency.

### What salary range can I expect after a Gen AI certification?

**Ans.** This varies significantly by role, company, city, and what you do with the credential. Professionals who complete a structured program, build a portfolio, and actively apply those skills in their current role or in a role switch report meaningful salary gains. Professionals who complete a course and treat it as a passive line item on a résumé typically see little change. The program is a tool; the outcome depends on how you use it.
