    answer: A 3-month course can teach the basics of Excel, SQL and one BI tool. Most job-ready programs take 6 months or more, because projects, GenAI skills and interview practice take time. A short course works best as a first step or a refresher.

## What Does a Data Analyst Do, and Which Analyst Roles Exist in India?

:::note
**Quick answer:** A data analyst turns raw business data into answers, using SQL to pull data, Excel or Python to clean it and Power BI or Tableau to present it. In India, business analyst roles are the most posted, followed by data, operations and BI analyst roles. Product, financial and marketing analysts are smaller, more specialised groups.
:::

A data analyst answers questions such as "Why did sales fall in Pune last quarter?" The work moves through four steps. You pull the data, clean it, find the pattern and explain it to the people who decide.

![Bar chart of analyst job types in India by share of live postings, led by business analyst at 48% and data analyst at 16.3%.](https://learnbay-s3.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/adlearnbay/DaIndiaAnalystJobTypes121126.webp)

_Share of live India analyst postings by job title. Source: Indeed India, 23 October 2026._

Here is what each role does day to day –

- **Business analyst** – links business needs to data and systems, writes requirements and tracks KPIs; the most common analyst title in India
- **Data analyst** – queries and cleans data, builds dashboards and reports, and answers ad hoc questions
- **Operations analyst** – tracks supply chain, service and process metrics, often in GCCs and logistics firms
- **BI analyst or developer** – builds and maintains Power BI or Tableau dashboards and the data models behind them
- **Financial analyst** – works on budgets, forecasts and risk numbers in banks, GCCs and finance teams
- **Product analyst** – studies how users behave in an app or website, common in startups and consumer tech
- **Marketing analyst** – measures campaigns, funnels and customer segments

Hence, a good data analytics course should prepare you for more than one of these titles, since most entry roles mix two or three of them. As business analyst is the most posted title, a program that teaches business analytics alongside data analytics widens your options.
