By 2026, the landscape of AI jobs in India has changed drastically. If you've been tracking job boards, LinkedIn updates, or recruiter messages, you've likely noticed a shift. The generic “software developer” roles that once dominated Indian hiring have quietly given way to a very different trend: <b>MLOps Engineer, Data Architect, AI Enterprise Product Manager, Cloud Security Architect, Generative AI (LLM) Engineer, and Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)</b>. Lately, the Forward Deployed Engineer role has entered the Indian job market, offering endless opportunities and setting new standards for AI hiring trends.
This isn't just a perception; it's a fact, supported by the data. Hiring trends from Naukri, LinkedIn, NASSCOM-Zinnov, and Glassdoor up to August 2026 reveal why Corporate India's recruitment looks different this year, which six roles are fueling the demand, and their actual salaries.

## Why Corporate India's Hiring Looks Different This Year

In FY2026, corporate India's white-collar hiring increased by 8%, marking the strongest three-year period on record. While this appears promising, it only tells part of the story. AI hiring trends in 2026 in the same industry tell an entirely different story, indicating 45% market growth, with consistent increases.
Today’s AI specialization has become essential for many working professionals across diverse domains. Professionals with generic technical expertise may limit their overall career growth compared to those who stay regularly updated.
In June 2026, overall IT job postings fell 3% year-on-year even as AI hiring within IT rose 16%; by July, IT postings had recovered to +6%.
The shift highlights the growing importance of specialized AI and technology skills in the Indian job market.

## The GCC Engine: India's Real Hiring Story

Global Capability Centres (GCCs) were once offshore support functions, but many now have built their own AI platforms, cloud architecture, and cybersecurity systems. Many even have full product engineering mandates.
In FY2026, GCCs out-hired traditional IT services firms by roughly 200,000 net employees, compared with IT services' ~110,000, up from a 1.1x hiring multiple in FY2025 to <b>1.8x</b> in FY2026.
There are now 2,117 GCCs in India operating across 3,728 business units, employing 2.36 million individuals. Of these centres, over 1,200 have integrated AI/ML, and about 250,000 AI professionals work within them.
In addition, around 64% of new AI positions were announced at GCC’s in 2026.

## The Demand-Supply Gap Behind the Hiring Boom

The demand for such premium positions requires specialized skills. However, there is also the factor of scarcity.
India currently has 257,600 core AI practitioners, while there are approximately 350,000 AI job openings during the same period. By 2027, the number of AI job vacancies could exceed 2.3 million, whereas the professional workforce is expected to be around 1.2 million. This gap will necessitate approximately 1.1 million additional AI experts to meet employment demand.
Even NASSCOM, with its conservative approach, estimated a deficit of 600,000 AI job vacancies.
India has a talent shortage of AI specialists with experience in shipping AI into production. Less than 5% of senior tech leaders report real-time experience in AI deployment. Only around two of the five companies perform large-scale AI deployment.
In 2026, we notice a widening talent supply gap and hybrid skill scarcity. The abrupt rise in demand for FDE roles triggers demand for AI-specialized IT engineers with unique client-facing and business consulting skills.

### a. MLOps Engineer

MLOps engineers combine machine learning, cloud, and DevOps to integrate model deployment, monitoring, retraining, and rollback processes, while supporting other business operations. In short, they bridge the gap between “training a model” and “getting ML into production.”
Reported median pay is ₹20,00,000, while senior specialists earn around ₹58,00,000. Entry-level MLOps roles are 17%-25% higher than DevOps roles, and the premium increases from there, whereas at the senior level it's more like 5%-13% higher.
DevOps, cloud, and backend engineers transition into this role, which suits their career aspirations.

### b. Data Architect

Data architects design the structures that let AI systems use data effectively.
Many failed AI projects come up because of data problems. Poorly organized data leads to unstable models and poor performance.
At ₹19 LPA, the median salary, and the salary of senior architects (7-9 years) at ₹36 LPA also indicate that this career path has great growth potential.
Data engineers, DBAs, and BI leads will find this an appealing path to crack AI jobs in India.

### c. AI/Enterprise Product Manager

AI/Enterprise product managers create AI-based products. They define model boundaries by creating an evaluation framework before shipping. They also determine whether the feature is a showcase feature or one that provides true value.
The reported median pay is ₹27 LPA. It should be noted that the measured premium over a traditional PM role is around 5%, rather than the quoted 20-50%. The larger value is for companies that approach you and at what level you are targeted.

### d. Cloud Security Architect

Cloud security architects create identity management, zero trust, compliance, and threat auto-response pipelines for support in the Azure cloud, now expanded to include services for data pipelines and AI models.
Regulatory compliance is the primary driver of hiring. The DPDP Act, RBI's FREE-AI framework, and SEBI guidelines have increased hiring for compliance. The median salary is around ₹20 LPA, and those with 10-14 years of experience earn around ₹48 LPA.
A design specialization, along with CCSP/CISSP, accelerates the careers of network engineers, systems administrators, and security analysts.

### e. Generative AI (LLM) Engineer

Engineers are building multiple applications with large language models (LLMs), fine-tuned domain models, RAG pipelines, and self-directed AI agents.
Engineers estimate that professionals developing these Generative AI/LLM applications earn approximately ₹9.9 lakh per year (LPA) on average. However, Senior GCC and Product company engineers earn in the range of ₹35 to 65 LPA if they build and deploy safe, live production LLM systems.
As of 2026, "Generative AI Engineer" salary estimates are lower than "ML Engineer", so there is almost no salary difference with the title. To justify a salary increase, engineers must build safe production LLM systems.
Building these systems also requires some programming, but not to the extent of full-scale programming. Working knowledge of RAG and context engineering, as well as fine-tuning, is required. As such, Data Scientists, Software Engineers, and ML Engineers are well positioned to enter this domain.

### f. Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)

This is the fastest-growing and latest title included in the list. Tech professionals should upskill to crack such exciting AI jobs in India.
Creating AI models and deploying them to production requires specific skills in both AI and CS. However, end-to-end hands-on AI work, meeting customer needs, and budgeting, along with working with data pipelines, requires business acumen and consulting skills.
This is the gap that the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role addresses. Unlike work done in product sandboxes, FDEs build, customize, and deploy AI and software systems in a client’s production environment.
FDE positions in India have grown by 729% YoY, and by mid-2026, it has become one of the fastest-rising searched roles in AI hiring on LinkedIn. The launch of OpenAI’s $4 billion Deployment Company subsidiary and its acquisition of Tomoro, along with FDE postings from McKinsey QuantumBlack and BCG X, demonstrate that AI frontier labs and the top consulting firms consider this hiring track one of their core focal areas, rather than a niche hiring track.
New IT services and AI-first startups job postings start at ₹18 LPA. More advanced positions in GCCs and MNCs can be as high as ₹70 LPA. Senior IT roles in frontier AI labs are ₹90+ LPA and beyond. The most skilled production experts who work in back-end, front-end, and client-facing full-stack solutions engineering are highly sought after.

No. Most people moving into these jobs have backgrounds in related fields such as DevOps, cloud infrastructure, data engineering, BI, and systems administration. They fill in the gaps in AI/ML through project-based learning rather than generic AI education.

### Which of the six roles pays the most?

After 8 years, AI/Enterprise Product Manager and GenAI Engineer are most likely to grow. Forward Deployed Engineer is the newest and least known of the six, but it has the highest growth rate and salary range across the AI labs.
